# 🏠 HOA Havoc

*A game by BigSpoke AI.*

A turn-based game of suburban survival. Your board is coming for your equity. Your neighbors know things — some of them are even telling the truth.

**Play it: https://farkaswork1970-sudo.github.io/hoa-havoc/**

## What it is

Describe your HOA — its size, its age, and five dials of board dysfunction — then survive it. Each month you spend action points and a communication budget on mowing, documenting, working overtime, and working your neighbors. The board plots in secret. Citations have deadlines. Appeals are always denied.

Win by voting the board out of existence at a recall meeting. Lose to foreclosure. Or sell the house and walk away on your own terms — those are the only three exits.

- **Hidden loyalties** — truthful friends, terrified fence-sitters, and deceivers who earn your trust before they lie. Expect about a year of chatter before you can sort the street.
- **Influence networks** — every neighbor sways a circle of households, and super-connectors float through all of them. Plant messages to win blocs before a vote.
- **Comparables** — every sale is tracked. Falling values drag your equity and radicalize the whole neighborhood against the board.
- **The management company** — every HOA's final boss. Rally enough allies to fire them and claw back every point they drained.
- **An optional AI assistant** — it advises you, but the board gets AI too.

Single self-contained HTML file. No dependencies, no build step, no tracking. Works offline. Runs on phones.

## Running it locally

Open `index.html` in any browser. That's the whole procedure.
