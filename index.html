⚙️ DESCRIBE YOUR HOA

Size sets how many neighbors you get, how many seats the board has, and how many votes a recall needs. Age sets how many absurd rules predate you, how decayed the commons start, how often the old infrastructure fails, and whether a developer's man or an entrenched founder runs the place.

Each one adds dues, wears the commons, and starts an argument you will be asked to take a side in.

🏷️ SELL THE HOUSE?

Nobody just leaves an HOA. There are exactly three exits: 🔨 foreclosure, 🎉 dissolving the board, or 🏷️ selling. This is the selling one — you walk away with your equity and your sanity, but the board stays in power. Neither victory nor defeat. Just… suburbia.
